Privacy Policy for Arrow Escape Quest

Last Updated: September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile game Arrow Escape Quest (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), developed and published by Core Nova Systems ("we", "our", or "us").

Privacy Summary: Arrow Escape Quest is built with privacy as a fundamental principle. We do not collect, store, share, sell, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) or device data. The application does not require any device permissions to operate.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect any personal information from its users. You are not required to create an account, register, log in, or provide any personal details (such as your name, email, phone number, address, or financial information) to play the game.

2. Device Permissions

Arrow Escape Quest operates completely without requesting any system permissions:

  • No Location access (GPS or network-based).
  • No Camera or Microphone access.
  • No Storage or Media access (Photos, Videos, Audio, Files).
  • No Contacts, Call Logs, or SMS access.
  • No Phone State or Device ID access.

3. Local Data Storage (SharedPreferences)

To provide a smooth puzzle-solving experience, the Application stores non-personal game progress locally on your device using Android's native SharedPreferences:

  • Game Progress: Highest level unlocked and star ratings achieved for completed levels.
  • User Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effects on/off, background music on/off) and haptic feedback settings.

All stored data remains strictly on your local device and is never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service. You can permanently erase all local progress at any time through the in-game Settings screen by selecting "Reset Progress", or by clearing the application data from your Android device settings.

4. Third-Party Libraries and SDKs

The Application contains no sensitive third-party software development kits (SDKs), advertising frameworks, tracking tools, or external analytics services. The application is built entirely using Google's official Android Jetpack and Jetpack Compose libraries.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Arrow Escape Quest complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
  • The Application does not feature behavioral advertising, personalized profiling, social media integrations, or chat functions.
  • If a parent or guardian believes that any information has been inadvertently provided, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly address the inquiry.

6. Security

Because the Application does not transmit or collect any personal information over the internet, your personal data is not exposed to online security vulnerabilities. All game preferences and level progress reside solely on your physical device.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage users to review this page periodically for any changes.
